🧱 Great Wall of China

The Great Wall of China is one of the world's most famous landmarks. It was constructed over many centuries and served defensive, transportation and border-control purposes.

📍 Location: China

🏗️ Built: Over many centuries

🌍 Category: New Seven Wonders of the World

💰 Trip Amount: ₹45,000 per person

🗓️ Trip Duration: 5 Days / 4 Nights

🏛️ Petra

Petra is an ancient city in southern Jordan, famous for its architecture carved into rose-colored sandstone. It is also known as the Rose City.

📍 Location: Jordan

🏛️ Known As: The Rose City

🌍 Category: New Seven Wonders of the World

💰 Trip Amount: ₹65,000 per person

🗓️ Trip Duration: 6 Days / 5 Nights

✝️ Christ the Redeemer

Christ the Redeemer is a famous statue in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. It stands on Corcovado Mountain and is one of the most recognizable landmarks in South America.

📍 Location: Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

🗿 Type: Monument / Statue

🌍 Category: New Seven Wonders of the World

💰 Trip Amount: ₹80,000 per person

🗓️ Trip Duration: 7 Days / 6 Nights

🏔️ Machu Picchu

Machu Picchu is an ancient Inca city located high in the Peruvian Andes. It is famous for its stone structures, mountain scenery and Inca history.

📍 Location: Peru

🏛️ Built By: Inca civilization

🌍 Category: New Seven Wonders of the World

💰 Trip Amount: ₹1,10,000 per person

🗓️ Trip Duration: 8 Days / 7 Nights

🏛️ Chichen Itza

Chichen Itza is an ancient Maya city in Mexico's Yucatan Peninsula. It is famous for the great pyramid known as El Castillo.

📍 Location: Yucatan, Mexico

🏛️ Civilization: Maya

🌍 Category: New Seven Wonders of the World

💰 Trip Amount: ₹95,000 per person

🗓️ Trip Duration: 7 Days / 6 Nights

🏟️ Colosseum

The Colosseum is an ancient amphitheatre in Rome, Italy. It was used for public events including gladiator contests and performances.

📍 Location: Rome, Italy

🏛️ Built: Roman Empire

🌍 Category: New Seven Wonders of the World

💰 Trip Amount: ₹55,000 per person

🗓️ Trip Duration: 5 Days / 4 Nights

🕌 Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a famous monument in Agra, India. It was commissioned by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

📍 Location: Agra, India

👑 Built By: Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan

❤️ Built In Memory Of: Mumtaz Mahal

🌍 Category: New Seven Wonders of the World

💰 Trip Amount: ₹25,000 per person

🗓️ Trip Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights
